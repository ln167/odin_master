World data representations: toys

Learning toys for the 3D game's data model. Each one is a 2D analogue you can poke: carve, break, zoom, and watch the memory cost. Notes and literature grades: docs/game/representations.md.

Recommended stack (proposed, not agreed)

  • World: one sampled distance field (9) holding shape, material, weakness and damage. Untouched terrain is generated from a formula; only edited regions are stored, as bricks.
  • Blocks, carvings, felled trees, fragments: each carries its own field plus a transform (A). They slide against the terrain field.
  • Breaking: min-cut through the weakness field (B / min-cut fracture); damage accumulates.
  • Loose rubble and dirt in motion: MPM particles (particles ↔ grid), settling back into the field.
  • Bulk earthworks (pyramid ramps): moving strata (F).
  • Ropes and dragging: particle chains with distance constraints, pinned to bodies (ropes toy). Needs a deterministic rigid-body + constraint solver; decided in the architecture phase.

Material decides how it breaks (bonded cell complex)

3D: rock (bonded cells, min-cut)Granite / sandstone / slate outcrops built from joint and bed planes; hammer chips, sledge plates, Roman plug-and-feather splitting. 3D: wood (fibre cells)Axe V-notch and chips, back cut, fall with torn hinge splinters; wedge splits along rays. Oak vs pine, green vs seasoned. 3D: sand / loam / clay (bonded grains)Same spade, three materials: sand pours to a cone, loam crumbles into clods, clay comes off as sharp spade spits and holds a scalloped wall.
