Particles: Lagrangian vs Eulerian

What it is

Both panels show the same swirling flow carrying the same stripes. On the left, 2000 particles each carry their own colour and mass (Lagrangian: data rides with the matter). On the right, a fixed grid stores colour per cell and the colour is passed from cell to cell (Eulerian: data stays put, matter flows through).

Lagrangian = a boat riding the river carrying its own sensor (the data moves with the matter). Eulerian = a buoy anchored in place measuring whatever flows past (the data stays put; matter moves through it).

Live stats

Particles are good at

  • Motion is free: just add velocity.
  • Identity survives: id and colour travel along.
  • No smearing: the pattern stays crisp.

Particles are bad at

  • Neighbour search needed every step.
  • No fixed home for queries ("what is at x?").
  • Density gets uneven (clumps, holes).

In 3D / for our game:

Rubble, crumbs and sand as particles that keep their id and material; a grid only to look up or summarise density.
