P1 · Field ↔ surface mesh

What it is

A field stores one number per grid point: negative inside the rock, positive outside (a signed distance field). Marching squares walks every cell, reads the signs of its 4 corners, and draws the line where the sign flips, which turns the field into an outline mesh. The reverse (a distance transform) rebuilds the field from that outline.

Controls

Click on the rock: carve. Shift-click: add rock.

Good at

  • Robust and simple: one table lookup per cell.
  • Local: an edit only needs its nearby cells re-extracted.
  • Works on any field, any shape or topology.

Bad at

  • Sharp corners get rounded (dual contouring fixes this by placing a vertex inside the cell).
  • Detail is capped by the grid; thin features vanish.
  • Saddle cells (5, 10) are ambiguous; the centre value breaks the tie.

In 3D / for our game

Marching cubes (256 cases) turns a voxel rock field into a triangle mesh we can render and collide with.
