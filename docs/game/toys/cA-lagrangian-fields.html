Lagrangian implicit fields

What it is

Terrain is one formula; every broken-off chunk carries its own small grid of signed distances (distance to the surface, negative inside) in its own body frame. Making a chunk a rigid body just means giving that grid a position and angle. Drawing and collision are only "ask each field how far is this point".

Controls

Stats

Good at

  • Promotion to rigid body = add a transform, no meshing.
  • Carving happens in body frame, the cut rotates with the matter.
  • Collision is plain field queries.

Bad at

  • Every fragment pays for its whole grid, even if mostly empty.
  • Overlapping fragments need a min over all of them.
  • Resolution is fixed per fragment (32×32 here).

In 3D / for our game: each rubble chunk would hold a small 3D distance volume (e.g. 32³×4 = 128 KB) plus a transform.
