Surface mesh

What it is

Points joined by edges (and in 3D, triangles) that trace only the skin of an object. The inside of the rock is not stored at all; it is just "the space the skin encloses".

Controls

Live stats

Good at

  • Compact, smooth or sharp surfaces
  • Render-ready as is
  • Cheap collision (test the skin only)

Bad at

  • No interior: no material, damage or cracks
  • Repeated cuts leave slivers and rounding error
  • Topology changes (splits, holes) are hard

In 3D / for our game

Good for the finished carved statue or block you render and collide with; not for deciding what a quarry cut reveals inside.
