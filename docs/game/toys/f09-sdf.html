f09 — Signed distance fields

What it is

A signed distance field (SDF) is a function that gives, for any point, the distance to the nearest surface, negative inside the rock and positive outside. "Analytic" means the function is a formula (a list of shapes); "sampled" means those distances are baked into a grid and interpolated. Carving and adding are just max and min of distances.

Good at

  • Unlimited resolution; edits compose (union, subtract).
  • Inside/outside, distance and normal from one query.

Bad at

  • Per-query cost grows with edit count.
  • No list of "parts"; rendering needs ray-marching or meshing.

In 3D / for our game

Same maths with spheres/boxes in 3D; a chunked baked grid is the fast cache, the op list is the cheap edit log.
