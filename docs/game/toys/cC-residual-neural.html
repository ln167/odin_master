C: residual neural field

What it is

A tiny neural network (a learned grid of numbers feeding a small formula) stores the whole hand-made world: shape, material and weakness. Your carves are stored as small "residual patches" (local correction grids added on top), so edits are instant. Folding re-trains the network to include the edits and frees the patches.

Click the big view to carve (radius 6 px).

Good at / Bad at

  • Good: compresses a hand-crafted multi-channel world below a dense grid.
  • Good: edits are cheap and exact immediately (just add a patch).
  • Good: one network answers shape, material and weakness together.
  • Bad: folding costs training compute (see fold ms).
  • Bad: folding can blur detail; patch-exact edits become approximate.
  • Bad: every query = network forward pass + sum of overlapping patches.
