Gaussian splats

What it is

A shape stored as thousands of unconnected soft blobs, each with a position, a size along two axes, a rotation, a colour and an opacity. The picture is made by blending the blobs directly, with no triangles or grid. Captured splat scenes only keep blobs near the visible surface.

Controls

Brush:

Drag on the rock.

Stats

Good at

  • Photoreal look from few parameters
  • No connectivity to maintain
  • Friendly to physics as particles (PhysGaussian 2024 ran MPM physics on splats)

Bad at

  • No inside/outside or solid query
  • Hollow by default; solid interiors need volume filling and many more splats
  • No explicit surface for collision

In 3D / for our game

3D splats carry spherical-harmonic colour (~236 B each); great for showing a quarry, but we would still need a separate solid model for digging and collision.
