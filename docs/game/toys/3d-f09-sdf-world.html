3D f09 — The world as one SDF

What it is

The whole 128×64×128 m world is one signed distance field ("goo"): negative inside rock, positive in air. Untouched terrain is a formula (noise hills + a granite outcrop + tilted strata), so it costs 0 bytes. Only edits are stored, as 8³-sample bricks that hold distance, damage and material. The picture is ray-marched straight from the field; your click is found by marching the same field on the CPU.

Controls

Click = apply tool at the surface. Left-drag = orbit, wheel = zoom, right-drag or shift-drag = pan.

Good at

  • Any shape: carve or add anywhere, any size, one rule (max / min).
  • Shape, material, weakness and damage come from one query at one point.
  • Untouched world is free; memory grows only with what players changed.

Bad at

  • No list of separate pieces: a cut-off chunk is still just "field", finding it needs a flood fill.
  • Rendering needs ray-marching or meshing; thin features below the sample spacing vanish.
  • Brick resolution caps edit detail (here 1 m).
