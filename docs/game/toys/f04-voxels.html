Regular voxels

What it is

The world is a uniform grid of equal cells (voxels = 3D pixels). A cell's neighbours are found by index arithmetic (x±1, y±1), never stored. Each cell just holds a value, here a material id.

Controls

Left drag carves. Right drag / shift+drag adds. Settle: cells not connected to the ground (flood fill) are tinted and fall straight down.

Live stats

Good at

  • Carving is flipping cells; topology change is free.
  • O(1) neighbours, no pointers.
  • Trivially parallel.

Bad at

  • Memory grows with volume, empty air included.
  • Blocky surfaces.
  • Same resolution everywhere.

In 3D / for our game:

Cost goes as n³: halving the cell size is 8× the memory, so a flat grid over 4 km cannot reach human-scale detail.
