3D P2G / G2P — Material Point Method

What it is

The 3D version of the 2D MPM toy. Matter is particles (each carries mass, velocity, a deformation matrix F and an affine velocity matrix C); a 32³ background grid is wiped and rebuilt every step. Each step: P2G spreads particle mass and momentum onto the 3×3×3 = 27 surrounding nodes, the grid adds gravity and walls, G2P reads velocities back and applies the material rule. MLS-MPM on the CPU in plain JS.

Sand uses Drucker-Prager friction: it can hold a slope (angle of repose) but has no cohesion. Water only resists volume change, so the same column spreads flat.

Controls

Camera: left-drag rotate, wheel zoom, right-drag or shift-drag pan. Press "Phase step" to walk through one MPM step. Hover a particle to inspect it.

Live stats

Good at

  • Sand, dirt, mud and water in one method; piles keep a repose angle.
  • Splitting, merging, digging: no mesh to repair.
  • Particles keep history (compaction, plastic flow).

Bad at

  • Cost: 27 nodes per particle twice per substep, plus a 3×3 eigen-solve per particle.
  • Needs ~8 particles per grid cell; a big pile is a lot of particles.
  • Settled matter still costs full price every step.

In 3D / for our game: run MPM only where matter is loose and moving (a dump, a dig, a collapse); once rubble settles, fold it back into the world field and delete the particles (mentioned here, not implemented).
