P4 - Min-cut fracture

What it is

The rock stores a weakness field (bedding planes = weak horizontal layers, joints = weak diagonal cracks) and an accumulated damage channel. Each impact asks: what is the cheapest set of bonds to cut that separates the hit zone from the ground (a min-cut, found with max-flow)? If that cost is below the impact energy, the cut happens and the piece falls - no physics solver involved.

Controls

Click to strike.

Live stats

Good at

  • Fracture shapes come from the geology for free: cracks run along bedding and joints.
  • No per-tool code: a tool is just energy + damage radius.
  • Deterministic, and damage persists, so many weak hits add up to one big break.

Bad at

  • Global max-flow is costly on big grids; needs a local window or coarse graph.
  • One cut per query - real shattering makes many pieces at once.
  • No momentum: pieces just fall, they are not thrown.

In 3D / for our game:

Run max-flow on a local voxel window around the hit, using the world field's weakness + damage channels as bond capacities.
