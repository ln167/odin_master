p2: voxels → graph → pieces → rigid bodies

What it is

A rock is a grid of cells (voxels). We promote it step by step: cells become a graph (cells linked to touching cells), a flood fill groups linked cells into pieces, and pieces not resting on the ground become simple rigid bodies (a lump with mass, centre of mass and spin).

Controls

Stage: click the pipeline bar above. Drag on the rock to use the brush.
Brush radius 2

Live stats

Good at

  • Robust: every step is simple and local.
  • Carving anything just edits cells; pieces fall out for free.
  • Easy to bench: each stage is a flat array.

Bad at

  • Cell size limits the shape (no smooth surfaces).
  • A rigid body is still voxels and needs meshing (see p1 toy).
  • Memory grows with volume, not surface.
