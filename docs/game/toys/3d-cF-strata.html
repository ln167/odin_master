3D layered strata terrain

What it is

A 96x96 grid of columns; each column is a stack of layers (material + thickness): bedrock, limestone, clay, sand, topsoil. Digging pops top layers into a basket, dumping pushes new layers on top, rain slides material downhill as layers. Mass is only moved, never created. 3D port of cF.

Controls

Click terrain to use the tool; ctrl-drag to paint. Left-drag rotate, wheel zoom, right/shift-drag pan.

Live stats

Good at

  • Geology-true strata, cut faces show stripes
  • Tiny memory vs dense voxels; mass easy to audit

Bad at

  • Still 2.5D: no overhangs or tunnels
  • Erosion here is a thermal slump, not real water flow

In 3D / for our game:

Settled dirt is cheap height layers instead of millions of particles; it falls back to MPM particles while it is moving, and to the SDF where overhangs or tunnels are needed (not implemented here).
