F02 Structured (grid-snapped) mesh

What it is

The rock is a mesh: points that may only sit on a square lattice (a regular grid), joined by edges we store explicitly. Each edge carries a bond strength, so a hit can snap individual bonds. It is not extracted from voxels: the edges are the data.

Good at

  • Regular spacing: neighbours by index maths, easy threading.
  • Explicit bonds that can break and leave pieces.

Bad at

  • Shape detail is limited to lattice spacing.
  • Staircase look unless smoothed.

In 3D / for our game:

Cubic lattice with diagonal edges: per-bond strength gives bedding planes and cracks that split a boulder into chunks.
