3D P4 - Min-cut fracture

What it is

A voxel rock carries a weakness field (tilted bedding planes and a few joint planes) plus an accumulated damage channel. An impact deposits damage; then a max-flow finds the cheapest set of bond faces separating the struck voxels from the anchored bottom layer (the min-cut). If that cost is below the impact energy, the cut happens and the piece falls. Cracks follow the geology; hits add up.

Live stats

Good at

  • Cracks run along bedding and joints for free; no per-tool fracture code.
  • Deterministic; damage persists, so many weak hits add up to one big break.
  • Cut faces are explicit, so the crack surface can be rendered and meshed.

Bad at

  • Global max-flow grows fast with voxel count; needs a local window or coarse graph.
  • One cut per query; real shattering makes many pieces.
  • Pieces just drop and tumble here; no momentum, no collision with the rock.

In 3D / for our game:

Detached pieces become objects that carry their own field + transform; crumbs become particles. In the real game run this on a local window around the hit using the world field's weakness + damage channels as capacities.
